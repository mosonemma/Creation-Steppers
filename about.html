Our Story

Who We Are

Empowering youth to transform communities through innovation, leadership, and advocacy.

Creation Steppers Youth Development Initiative (CSYDI) is a community-based organization dedicated to building skills, creativity, and empowerment among young people in Uganda. Through art, education, and social action, we inspire transformation and sustainable impact.

Purpose

Our Core Objectives

Creation Steppers is established to achieve strategic objectives that drive our mission and community work.

Education & Child Rights

Promote child rights, education, and healthy development for all young people.

Health & Hygiene

Promote hygiene, sanitation, and HIV/AIDS prevention through life skills training.

Girl Child Advocacy

Advocate for girl child human rights and provide capacity building for vulnerable youth.

Economic Empowerment

Build self-reliance through job creation projects and sustainable socio-economic development.

Environmental Protection

Preserve the environment through tree planting and promote environmental conservation.

Governance & Peace

Promote civic education, good governance, and peace reconciliation in our communities.

How We Work

Partnerships: Collaborate with international bodies, governments, and organizations to advance our mission.

Resources: Accept donations, grants, subscriptions, and support from all sources to fund our programs.

Awareness: Conduct public campaigns, publications, and outreach to promote our objectives.

Development: Acquire property, construct facilities, and employ experts to support our operations.

Milestones

Our Journey

2011

Formation

Started as Creation Steppers Music Empire to use art for community transformation.

2015

Expansion

Introduced drama and dance projects addressing social issues like FGM and early marriages.

2018

Official Registration

Became Creation Steppers Youth Development Initiative (CSYDI), expanding to skills training and advocacy.

2023

Community Impact

Launched new projects focusing on innovation, digital skills, and sustainability across Uganda.

2024

Music Studio Expansion

Established a dedicated music studio to strengthen youth talent development while advancing HIV/AIDS awareness, back-to-school initiatives, and campaigns against early child marriage and FGM.

2025

Annual River Carnival

Strengthened the flagship Annual River Carnival, creating a platform for youth from diverse communities to socialize, learn from one another, and engage in edutainment.

2026

Annual Excursion Initiative

Launched multi-location excursions to enable youth to explore diverse skills, develop talents, and gain experiential learning opportunities beyond their home communities.

Mid-2026

School and Community Engagement

Through our S!MAMA! initiative, we have strengthened support for school-based clubs and drama groups across multiple schools during the first half of the year. The programme has also expanded into communities through cinema outreach sessions that use film and discussion to raise awareness about the harmful effects of negative traditional practices and promote positive social change.
