About Us

Creation Steppers Youth Development Initiative (CSYDI) started in 2011 as a music, dance, and drama group formerly known as Creation Steppers Music Empire (CSME), promoting positive community change. In 2018, it was registered as Creation Steppers Youth Development Initiative (CSYDI) to support more youth talents and development programs aimed at addressing issues like FGM, early marriages, GBV, HIV/AIDS, and drug misuse through advocacy and creativity.

Community Impact

Advocacy · Amudat District

Simama! Say NO to Harmful Practices

CSYDI has launched a 3-month, student-led project tackling child marriage, FGM, and gender-based violence across Loroo and Abiliyep Sub-counties. Through school clubs, community dialogues, drama, and media campaigns, students and residents are working together to protect the rights of adolescent girls.

Partnership · Soroti

Community Influencers Summit on Ending FGM

CSYDI joined the Community Influencers Empowerment Summit hosted by Naguru Teenage Centre in Soroti, bringing together advocates to explore storytelling and community engagement strategies for ending FGM.
