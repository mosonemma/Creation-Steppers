Governance & Policies

🧑‍💼 Human Resource Policy

We are committed to fair, transparent, and inclusive human resource practices. Our organization promotes equal opportunity in recruitment, professional development, and staff welfare. We ensure a safe and respectful working environment free from discrimination, harassment, and exploitation. Staff are guided by clear roles, performance expectations, and a code of conduct that reflects our values and mission.

💰 Finance Policy

We uphold strong financial management systems to ensure accountability and transparency in all our operations. All funds are managed responsibly, with proper budgeting, documentation, and reporting procedures in place. Regular financial reviews and controls are implemented to prevent misuse of resources and to ensure funds are used effectively to achieve our organizational goals.

🛒 Procurement Policy

Our procurement processes are guided by fairness, transparency, and value for money. We follow clear procedures when sourcing goods and services, ensuring competitive selection and proper documentation. We are committed to ethical procurement practices that prevent conflicts of interest and promote integrity in all transactions.

✈️ Travel Policy

We ensure that all official travel is planned, approved, and conducted in a cost-effective and safe manner. Travel arrangements are made based on necessity and organizational priorities, with clear guidelines on expenses, accommodation, and reporting. Staff are expected to maintain professionalism and accountability during all official trips.

🛡️ Safeguarding & Community Protection

We are committed to protecting children, youth, and vulnerable individuals from harm, abuse, and exploitation. Our programs promote dignity, respect, and safety, and we actively work to eliminate harmful cultural practices such as FGM. All staff and partners are expected to uphold safeguarding standards at all times.

Full policy documents are available upon request for partners, donors, and stakeholders.
